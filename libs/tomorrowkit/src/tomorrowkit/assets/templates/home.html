What a patent is trying to do

A patent can protect a specific piece of technical territory: a claimed invention that the application describes and enables. In exchange for teaching the public how the invention works, an issued patent may provide a limited right to exclude others from practicing the claimed territory.

What matters early is not polished legal writing. It is evidence and dates: a complete, honest description of what you invented, when you had it, and what was already out in the world. That record is what this workspace helps you build.

From idea to application

These are different things, and the order matters. Each one builds on the record of the one before it.

  1. Idea

    A direction in your head. Not yet protectable — but worth capturing before it fades or leaks.

  2. Invention disclosure

    A written account of the problem, how your invention works, and what makes it different. Complete enough that someone skilled in your field could follow it.

  3. Provisional application

    A temporary, unexamined filing that can establish an early priority date for subject matter it adequately describes. A later application generally must claim its benefit within twelve months.

  4. Examined application

    A later non-provisional or international application with formal claims. A claim receives the provisional's earlier date only to the extent the provisional supports it.

Words you will meet

Priority date
The earliest effective filing date a claim may be entitled to. Whether a reference counts as prior art depends on the claim, the disclosure, and applicable law.
Disclosure
What the application actually teaches in writing and drawings. Later claims need adequate support in that record.
Claims
The numbered sentences in a later application that define the exact boundary of protection. Provisionals don't require them.
Provisional application
A temporary, unexamined filing that can establish an early priority date for adequately described subject matter.
Non-provisional application
A later application that can be examined. To claim a provisional's benefit, it generally must be filed within twelve months.
PCT
An international route that keeps foreign filing options open from a single application.
New matter
Anything you add after filing. It doesn't get the earlier date — which is why completeness now beats polish later.

How this workspace is laid out

Each invention gets one matter workspace with connected areas. You and your AI fill them together; every artifact exists to make the next decision easier.

Matter home

Where things stand: current stage, what's known, what's uncertain, and the next action.

Invention brief

The evolving account of the problem, mechanism, result, alternatives, and open questions — in your words.

Harvesting room

Guided sessions with your own AI to pull the full invention out of your head, checkpoint by checkpoint.

Invention map

A visual canvas of components, steps, alternatives, and open questions. For thinking — not a formal patent drawing.

Reference library

Prior art, papers, products, and leads — organized, tagged, and marked by how far each has been verified.

Decision ledger

The important calls you make, and why. Six months from now you'll be glad it's written down.

Scorecard

Two separate lenses on readiness — the value of the terrain, and how well the record captures it.

Export

A portable package of everything above. Yours to keep or hand to counsel.

Your role when working with AI

AI is a strong drafting partner and a tireless researcher, but the inventor is you. On anything important, your job has four verbs: understand what was produced, select what belongs in the record, modify what isn't quite right, and approve it explicitly. The decision ledger exists to capture those approvals.

Three moments that matter

Moment one

Before telling the world

Public demos, launches, papers, and pitches can start unforgiving clocks — and in much of the world, end your options. Capture and consider filing first.

Moment two

Before filing the provisional

A later claim receives the provisional's earlier date only for subject matter the provisional adequately supports. This is the moment to make the record complete, not pretty.

Moment three

Before the conversion decision

Around month twelve you decide whether to file a later application claiming the provisional's benefit. A well-kept matter record makes that a business decision instead of a scramble.
